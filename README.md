# FlickCut

I got tired of scrubbing through hours of gameplay footage hunting for that one insane
kill — so I'm building FlickCut: a gaming screen recorder that edits my highlights
automatically, the moment they happen.

## How it works

I play. FlickCut watches my inputs — every mouse click, every controller button — and when
something epic happens, it cuts a stylish edit right there: slow motion on the kill,
speed curves bending around the action, zoom punches on the clutch. No timeline surgery.

## Inspiration

💡 A personal shout-out from me (Taha): massive respect to [Recordly](https://recordly.dev/) —
for proving gameplay deserves an automatic highlight reel — and to
[OpenScreen](https://github.com/siddharthvaddem/openscreen) — the open-source screen
recorder that captures like it means it. 🎩
